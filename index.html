Research

Adaptive Reference Galleries for Camera Trap Classification In progress

With Dr. Lukas Picek · Beery Lab, MIT · 2026–present

Camera-trap species classifiers often break when they are moved to a new site, with new backgrounds, lighting and species. I'm building a way to adapt them without any retraining: a frozen vision foundation model plus a reference gallery of examples that updates itself as new images come in.

Deep Generative Model based Rate-Distortion for Image Downscaling Assessment

Yuanbang Liang, Bhavesh Garg, Paul Rosin, Yipeng Qin
CVPR 2024 · Oral (top 0.78%)

There was no good way to measure how much detail an image-downscaling method keeps. Our idea: a better small image should give a better large image when you upscale it back. We use generative super-resolution models to turn this into IDA-RD, a rate–distortion score for downscaling methods.

Paper arXiv Code

Perceptual cGAN for MRI Super-Resolution

Sahar Almahfouz Nasser, Saqib Shamsi, Valay Bundele, Bhavesh Garg, Amit Sethi
IEEE EMBC 2022

Sharper brain MRI from low-resolution scans. A 3D conditional GAN is trained with a perceptual loss from a 3D VGG-style network pre-trained on MRI classification, and a projection discriminator conditioned on the low-resolution input.

Paper

LiDAR Depth Supervision for Camera-only 3D Object Detection

FiveAI, Cambridge · Research Engineer intern · 2022

Self-driving cars often have LiDAR when the model is trained, but camera-only models have to guess depth on the road. I studied how depth from LiDAR point clouds can supervise a Lift-Splat-Shoot detector, and improved FiveAI's in-house camera-only detector by 20% mAP.
